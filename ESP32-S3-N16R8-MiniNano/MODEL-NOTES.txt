All dimensions millimeters, scale 1:1. Exact manufacturer schematic and revision not identified.
Not Arduino Nano, Waveshare Nano or ESP32-S3 SuperMini; their dimensions/pinouts are not substituted.
PCB dimensioned 22.40 wide x 31.96 long. Thickness 1.20 estimated.
Orientation component-side up: antenna at -Z, USB at +Z; X increases left-to-right.
Four columns of 11 positions: A outer-left, B inner-left, C inner-right, D outer-right.
Reference IDs A01..D11 are local model IDs, not MCU package numbers or Arduino D aliases.

With-header envelope therefore 22.86 x 33.46 x 12.94 (all header heights estimated).
N16R8 naming implies 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM; verify actual parts and memory initialization.
GPIO26 and GPIO33..37 are exposed in chart but overlap flash/PSRAM buses, particularly octal PSRAM.
Do not use these as spare I/O on an N16R8/R8 configuration without confirmed schematic and memory routing.
TX/RX are interpreted as default UART0 GPIO43/44; the back photo labels IO43 TX / IO44 RX.
An RGB-like LED and both ceramic and external RF connectors are visible; LED GPIO and RF selection
network are unverified. Do not assume the larger white board's GPIO48 LED wiring or antenna switching.
VIN voltage range, regulator output current, USB backfeed protection and power paths are unverified.
Tracks, solder, QFN leads, antenna matching, silkscreen and exact USB/RF contacts omitted.
Pinout.json and the reference PDF describe all 44 positions and limitations.
