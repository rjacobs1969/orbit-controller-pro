Orbit Controller Pro - ergonomic outer-shell concept

STATUS: concept / ergonomic study, NOT a production-ready enclosure.
All CAD dimensions are millimeters, scale 1:1.

This is an original housing design using the supplied SpaceMouse Pro and
Enterprise photos as ergonomic references. It is not a dimensional copy of
either commercial product. Original Hackman3D files were used only to compare
envelopes: PalmRest approximately 128 x 207 x 30.5; base 53 x 83 x 24.
The old housing, electronics mount and original joystick interface are not reused.

Confirmed arrangement, from the rear toward the user's palm:
1. Landscape 2.79-inch NV3007 display.
2. Two rows of four Kailh Choc switches.
3. JH16 stick with a proposed rounded grip.
4. Broad, gently declining palm support.

FORM AND LAYOUT
Approximate shell envelope: 138.5 wide x 220 long x 51.8 high.
Grip top approximately 53 high. The display eyebrow and control shoulders
blend into a narrow waist and a low, flared palm-support nose.
Shell center height transitions from approximately 46 near the rear through
28 at the stick and 20/15 across the palm area to 6 at the front edge.
Longitudinal sides and cross-sections use smooth B-spline loft surfaces.

Layerling X is left/right and internal Z is the long axis:
rear is negative Z, user's palm/front is positive Z.
STEP uses Z-up, with its Y direction opposite Layerling's internal Z.
Display center: X 0, internal Z -78; screen tilted 20 degrees toward the user.
The actual previously modeled display STEP is included as a reference:
25.90 x 78.00 x 6.00 with the already-documented estimated layer heights.
Display active-area opening: 65.40 x 22.50, with a raised rounded bezel.

Switch centers: X -28.50, -9.50, +9.50, +28.50; rows at internal Z -45 and -25.
Column pitch 19; row pitch 20. Actual earlier Choc model geometry is reused.
Proposed keycaps: 17.50 x 16.50 x 3.00, with rounded edges.
Nominal square shell openings 14.20 x 14.20 are concept locations, not a
validated snap-in plate. Switch deck datum heights are 37.00 and 32.40.
The closest neutral grip-to-keycap plan-view gap is approximately 6.50.
Finger reach, travel, shaft tilt and keycap interference need physical testing.

Stick center: X 0, internal Z +12.
JH16 housing and shaft are simplified dimensional references, not a mount.
Housing underside height 14; nominal shaft top 32.95.
Proposed grip: 44 diameter at its lower envelope, 36 at its upper envelope,
24 high, rounded edges and a nominal 4.20 diameter, 6 deep socket.
Its attachment, shaft flats, strength and full joystick tilt clearance are
not engineered. The shell's 10 diameter shaft opening is provisional.

FILES
Orbit-Ergonomic-Concept.lyl: editable Layerling layout with separate shell,
lower plate, display, eight switches/keycaps and stick/grip references.
Orbit-Ergonomic-Shell.step/.stl/.3mf: shell and lower plate ONLY.
Orbit-Ergonomic-Layout.step/.3mf: complete concept including component references.
Preview PNGs show the layout; they are not additional physical parts.

CONSTRUCTION LIMITS
Hollow upper concept skin with nominal 3.20 coordinate inset. This is not an
exact constant-normal-thickness shell; local lofted wall thickness varies.
Lower concept plate is 2.40 high, with a 0.30 nominal separation from the upper.
End closure, split-line behavior and fit are not manufacturing specifications.
No fasteners, screw bosses, display supports, joystick bracket, button PCB,
electronics carrier, USB opening, cable routing, ballast or rubber feet are
engineered. The lower plate is not retained by anything yet.
Component reference colors and knob/keycap shapes are illustrative.
The concept exports do not establish printability, comfort or hand-size fit.
Make a low-cost ergonomic mockup and measure actual components before
developing the enclosure's detailed mounting and fastening design.
